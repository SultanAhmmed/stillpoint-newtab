# Privacy Policy for Stillpoint New Tab

**Last updated: September 22, 2026**

Stillpoint New Tab ("Stillpoint", "we", "our", or "the extension") is a Chrome extension designed to provide a calm and customizable new tab experience with search, quick shortcuts, backgrounds, and a focus timer.

We believe privacy should be simple. Stillpoint does not require an account and does not use analytics, advertising trackers, or user tracking.

## 1. Information We Collect

Stillpoint does **not** collect or transmit personally identifiable information.

We do not collect:

* Names
* Email addresses
* Passwords or authentication information
* Financial or payment information
* Health information
* Precise location information
* Browsing history
* Search history
* Keystrokes
* Mouse activity
* Analytics or tracking data

Stillpoint does not operate a backend server for collecting user information.

## 2. Information Stored Locally

Stillpoint uses the Chrome Storage API to save settings and personalization so that your preferences remain available when you open a new tab.

Depending on the features you use, locally stored information may include:

* Selected search engine
* Website shortcuts
* Shortcut settings and ordering
* Focus timer preferences
* Background preferences
* Other extension settings

This information is stored locally through Chrome's storage facilities and is not sent to Stillpoint or to a Stillpoint server.

## 3. Custom Background Images

Stillpoint allows users to select or upload their own background images for personalization.

Custom background images are used only to display the user's chosen background within the extension.

Stillpoint does not upload these images to a Stillpoint server or use them for analytics, advertising, or profiling.

## 4. Search

Stillpoint allows users to choose a preferred search engine, including supported search providers such as DuckDuckGo, Brave Search, Google, Bing, Startpage, and Ecosia.

When a user performs a search, the search request is sent directly to the search provider selected by the user.

Stillpoint does not operate the search service and does not collect or store the user's search queries.

The selected search provider may process search requests according to its own privacy policy and terms. Users should review the privacy policy of their chosen search provider for information about how that provider handles search requests.

## 5. Notifications

Stillpoint requests the Chrome `notifications` permission so that the focus timer can provide desktop notifications when a timer session reaches its configured endpoint.

Notifications are used only for extension functionality and are not used for advertising or tracking.

## 6. Data Sharing

Stillpoint does not sell, rent, or transfer user data to third parties.

Stillpoint does not use user data for advertising, profiling, creditworthiness, lending, or unrelated purposes.

The extension may open external websites, such as the search provider selected by the user. Those websites operate independently and have their own privacy policies.

## 7. Data Security

Because Stillpoint does not maintain a server containing user information, there is no Stillpoint database containing personal user profiles or browsing histories.

Users are responsible for the security of their own browser profile and device.

## 8. Children's Privacy

Stillpoint does not knowingly collect personal information from children or other users.

The extension does not require users to create an account or provide personal information.

## 9. Changes to This Privacy Policy

We may update this Privacy Policy if the functionality or privacy practices of Stillpoint change.

When changes are made, the "Last updated" date at the top of this policy will be updated accordingly.

Users are encouraged to review this page periodically for any changes.

## 10. Contact

If you have questions about this Privacy Policy or Stillpoint New Tab, you can contact us through the project's GitHub repository:

**GitHub:** https://github.com/sultanahmmed/stillpoint-newtab

## 11. Summary

In short:

* No account is required.
* No personal information is collected by Stillpoint.
* No browsing history is collected.
* No search history is collected by Stillpoint.
* No analytics or tracking is used.
* Settings are stored locally using Chrome Storage.
* Custom backgrounds are used locally for personalization.
* Timer notifications are used only for focus-timer functionality.
* Search requests go directly to the search provider selected by the user.
* Stillpoint does not sell or transfer user data.
